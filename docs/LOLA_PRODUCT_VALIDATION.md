# LOLA PDS product validation record

## Product chosen

- Product: `LDEM_85S_40M` (40 m/pixel south-polar LOLA gridded shape/elevation map).
- PDS data set: `LRO-L-LOLA-4-GDR-V1.0`; producer: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center / LRO LOLA team.
- Official archive directory: [PDS Geosciences LOLA polar IMG directory](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lro/lro-l-lola-3-rdr-v1/lrolol_1xxx/DATA/LOLA_GDR/polar/img/).
- Files: [`ldem_85s_40m.img`](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lro/lro-l-lola-3-rdr-v1/lrolol_1xxx/DATA/LOLA_GDR/polar/img/ldem_85s_40m.img), [detached `.lbl`](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lro/lro-l-lola-3-rdr-v1/lrolol_1xxx/DATA/LOLA_GDR/polar/img/ldem_85s_40m.lbl), and [PDS4 XML label](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/lro/lro-l-lola-3-rdr-v1/lrolol_1xxx/DATA/LOLA_GDR/polar/img/ldem_85s_40m.xml).
- The binary and both labels are included under `data/lola/` in this package. The validated IMG SHA-256 is `1609e9b860748e0b9952a7239c51b37d3b2a799d35e9fc3c258526711cc1e51d`; the PDS3 LBL SHA-256 is `d8105776af7fb79b1862ffa3946d013ec34f4dc6a00b7f6bff22ef8f768a5d3d`; and the PDS4 XML SHA-256 is `e246e7bcb6773a6a114d0fb17760655e64b46a8b4afc15ea67eb98c5820e58ff`. Reproducible validation metrics are in `data/lola/ldem_85s_40m_validation.json`.

## Verified from the downloaded PDS3 label and PDS4 label

- Sample type: little-endian signed integer (`LSB_INTEGER`), 16 bits/sample; image is 7,584 lines × 7,584 samples, with 40 m/pixel polar stereographic map scale.
- Coverage metadata: 85°S through 90°S; planetocentric latitude; positive-east longitude. A requested horizon radius that extends past this raster or intersects an invalid sample must fail, not return a partial profile.
- Horizontal frame: body-fixed rotating `MEAN EARTH/POLAR AXIS OF DE421`; spherical lunar axes 1,737.4 km; south-polar stereographic projection centered at 90°S / 0°E.
- Height scaling: the product label's explicit note says `HEIGHT = DN × SCALING_FACTOR`; `SCALING_FACTOR = 0.5`, `UNIT = METER`. The label separately says `PLANETARY_RADIUS = DN × SCALING_FACTOR + OFFSET`, where `OFFSET = 1,737,400 m` is the reference-sphere radius. The converter therefore stores **relative height in metres as `DN × 0.5`** and does not add the radius offset to height.
- Vertical reference: height is relative to the 1,737,400 m reference sphere, not a geoid-corrected orthometric surface. PDS documentation says geoid-relative topography requires subtracting a geoid radius from planetary radius; no such geoid correction is applied here.
- Product label describes a shape map made from LOLA altimetry, generated by interpolation/surface methods, and reports that some fraction is interpolated. The 40 m grid is not a direct per-pixel laser measurement and is not a certified landing-safety surface.
- Observed raw DN extrema matched the label exactly: `-11002` to `14054`; converted relative-height extrema are `-5501.0` to `7027.0` m. The generated GeoTIFF SHA-256 is `107eec2bfd1cd5c127320fe05e5ee5175cdb13cd94ed98ea68b396f18a63abca`.

The product-specific converter `scripts/prepare_lola_85s_40m.py` checks the detached label against expected dimensions, integer representation, scale, offset, polar projection, coverage and frame; verifies IMG byte count and actual DN extrema against the label; then writes a lunar-CRS GeoTIFF with the documented height scale. It deliberately refuses different labels instead of applying generic LOLA assumptions.

## What this validation does not establish

This verifies the archive product and the conversion semantics; it does **not** verify that any frontend candidate latitude/longitude is the exact coordinate of a landing-site ROI or is expressed in the product's DE421 mean-Earth frame. The inspected frontend candidate records are explicitly unverified. Do not set the site frame or activate live terrain analysis until those coordinates and their frame are independently established. No transformation between distinct lunar frames is implemented.

A processing-only smoke test at the reference archive's unverified candidate coordinate (-89.9°, 0°) produced a full profile at a 10 km requested radius. A 50 km request at its other unverified candidate (-85.9°, 0°) was correctly refused at the -85° coverage edge (first uncovered sample at 27.28 km). These are tests of raster processing and coverage rejection only, not validated site results.

The algorithm's complete-ray coverage guard checks every requested sample for raster bounds and nodata. Terrain sampling remains a prototype: no independent horizon reference comparison, local elevation uncertainty propagation, slope/boulder hazard analysis, or mission-grade validation has been performed.
